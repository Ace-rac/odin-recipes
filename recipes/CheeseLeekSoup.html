Cheese and Leek soup with Minced meat



Home

Description

This is the ideal recipe if you want to serve a creamy and flavorful soup to any gathering in just 20 minutes.

  • 2 small Baguettes
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 500g minced meat, mixed
  • n.B. salt and pepper
  • 3 poles Leek
  • 700ml water
  • 3 vegetable stock cubes
  • 250g processed cheese
  • 1 cup Crème fraîche
  • n.b. nutmeg
  • n.b. garlic powder
  • n.b. onion powder

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (top/bottom heat) and bake the baguettes for about 10 minutes
  2. Meanwhile, heat oil in a large pot. Brown the ground meat on all sides until crumbly and season with salt and pepper. Clean the leek, slice it into rings, and add it to the ground meat. Sauté for about 5 minutes. Pour in water, add the boullion cude, and simmer over low heat for about 10 more minutes.
  3. Reduce the heat significantly or briefly remove the pot from the heat before stirring in the processed cheese and crème fraîche. Do not allow it to boil vigorously, so that the soup remains creamy. If the heat is too high, the protein will curdle and the fat will dissolve into the emulsion.
  4. Season the soup generously with salt, pepper, nutmeg, garlic powder and onion powder.
  5. Slice the baguettes and serve them with the soup.
